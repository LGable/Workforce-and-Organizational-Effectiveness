# Contract Labor Resource Review

**Type:** Professional narrative + synthetic staffing scenario.

In a prior inpatient role, the owner reports reviewing approximately three months of staffing utilization and contractor contributions to prepare a leadership recommendation regarding lower-value contract staffing hours. A recommendation to reduce hours is not the same as payroll savings unless changed hours and actual loaded costs are documented.

## Example model

Potential expense reduction = (baseline billed hours − actual billed hours) × contracted hourly rate − transition/coverage expense. Check that patient coverage and safety remained adequate, and account for hours shifted to other personnel.

`contract_staffing_synthetic.csv` illustrates 12 weeks of fictional staffing time; no employer schedule or contractor-identifying information is included.
