# Staff Training and Competency Implementation

**Type:** Historical implementation summary, with fictional training-record demonstration.

At a school-based clinical organization, the owner reports coordinating sensory protocol education for **27 staff**, documenting **15 successful return demonstrations**, and establishing **seven classroom-level implementation owners**. This demonstrates cross-functional rollout, competency observation and ongoing ownership.

## Measurement design

Distinguish attendance from competency assessment: not all attendees necessarily received an assessment. Never calculate 15/27 as a pass rate. Record **assessed count, pass count, retraining count, follow-up fidelity** separately.

Proposed Kirkpatrick-style framework: session attendance; short knowledge check; return-demonstration skill; workplace implementation follow-through; maintenance assessment.

The sample `training_synthetic.csv` is completely fictional and does not represent real participants. It is not evidence of clinical efficacy of any particular intervention.
