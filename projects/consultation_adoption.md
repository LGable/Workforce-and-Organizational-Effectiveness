# Consultation Implementation & Change Adoption

**Type:** Owner-reported improvement portfolio; measurement needs verification.

The owner introduced Asana task tracking for clinical documents, milestones and protocol rollout, plus Slack communication patterns for post-consult follow-up. Preliminary reported observations: three fewer missed tasks and a **72% improvement in effective protocol implementation** after consultation.

## Measurement specification to adopt before public quantification

- Numerator: eligible recommendations implemented and confirmed within agreed window.
- Denominator: eligible recommendations requiring action; exclude not applicable or clinically superseded items with reason.
- Baseline/follow-up periods: pre-specified equivalent windows.
- Change metric: distinguish relative increase ((after-before)/before) from percentage-point difference (after-before).
- Monitor implementation *quality* separately from acknowledgment and task completion.
- Safety: avoid collecting student/patient identifiers into nonapproved systems.

The historical 72% is **not an independently verified causal effect** and should not be reconstructed into an invented sample size.
